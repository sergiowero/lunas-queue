Right now each tab's filters live only inside that page, so they're lost when you navigate away. The tabs with filters are Cola (project), En curso (project) and Completadas (project, PR state and the page of the "resto" list).

The options I gave you:
- **Per tab, in the URL and the browser session:** each tab remembers its own filters, and they survive a page reload.
- **Project shared across tabs:** one project filter applies to every tab, and Completadas keeps its PR-state filter to itself.
- **Per tab, in memory only:** filters are remembered while the app is open, and a reload resets them.
- **Per tab, in local storage:** filters survive even closing and reopening the browser.
