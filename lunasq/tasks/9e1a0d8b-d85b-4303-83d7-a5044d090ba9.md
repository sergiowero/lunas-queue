The spec for task #67 (adding additional instructions when creating a task) isn't submitted yet; it's waiting on one question I sent to you through LunasQ.

The question is which agents receive the instructions and whether they can be edited after the task is created. The options are:
- **All phases, editable (recommended):** refinement, implementation and verification all get them, and they can be changed like the goal while the task isn't finished.
- **All phases, creation only:** every phase gets them, but they can't change after the task is created.
- **Refinement only, editable:** only the refiner sees them and folds them into the spec.

Whichever you pick, the plan is an optional free-text field (up to 10,000 characters) on the new-task form. It's stored on the task separately from the goal, shown in the task detail and sent to agents as `task.instructions`.

Once your answer comes back, I'll write the spec with that choice and submit it with `submit_spec`.
